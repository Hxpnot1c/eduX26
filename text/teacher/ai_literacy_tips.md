# AI-literacy tips on the teacher's student review (decision 0030)

One tip per skill and level, shown under the bar. Each line is `skill.level: tip`. Teacher-facing wording.

- ask.new: Ask them to say what they already know before they ask the chatbot, then ask one narrower question.
- ask.developing: Ask them to name what a good answer would need to include before they ask.
- ask.secure: Ask them to show a classmate how they turn a broad question into a focused one.
- verify.new: Ask them to find one claim in a chatbot answer and check it against their notes or textbook.
- verify.developing: Ask them to check a figure or date in a chatbot answer against a named source.
- verify.secure: Ask them to explain how they decided which source to trust when two disagreed.
- how_ai_works.new: Talk through why a chatbot can sound sure and still be wrong.
- how_ai_works.developing: Ask them when a chatbot's knowledge stops, and what that means for recent data.
- how_ai_works.secure: Ask them to explain to the class why a chatbot may agree with whatever they suggest.
