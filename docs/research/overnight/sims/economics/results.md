## 2. Break-even and price points (5 credits/call, PAYG)

- One call costs the school £0.0375.
- Microsoft's take equals the £5 licence at 133 calls a year (4.4 a week over 30 weeks); the £3 trust tier at 80 (2.7 a week).
- To keep the whole bill at or under Sparx's top estimate (£2.80) **with eduX free**, a student could make only 75 calls a year (2.5 a week). At the £3 trust tier, none: £3 alone is above £2.80.

- A pack beats PAYG only when at least 82% of it is used.
- Central usage spread over 6.9 active months is 433 credits per student-month: about 58 students fill one 25k pack, and 47 reach its break-even. Use is seasonal (holidays, exam leave), so a trust needs a margin above that; the pool is shared with other agents.
- Best credible price cut is about 20% (pre-purchase, 'up to'). No education discount was found (business plan §3). Credit price is not the lever; call count is.
