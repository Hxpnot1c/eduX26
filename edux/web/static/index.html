Synapse

About

A plain record of what has been practised, so your chatbot can teach what comes next.

Synapse sits behind a student's own chatbot. It remembers which parts of a course have been practised, how well, and how long ago, and tells the chatbot what to do next. These pages let you see that record.

Where to look

The course map: every topic, concept and part, and how they depend on one another.

My progress: for students, the same map coloured by what you have practised.

For teachers: how a class is getting on, never one student's record.

How to read the colours

    Colour only ever means one thing: how well a part is known so far. The ring and fill say the same, so nothing depends on seeing colour.

    Practice feedback, not a grade. Synapse shows what has been practised and how recently, and it is often based on very few answers.
